# ChargePath outcomes

- [ ] Create a beautiful and creative responsive website for an Electric Vehicle Charging Station Planner.
- [ ] Let the planner use trip distance, current battery level, charging time, and station availability when recommending a charging station.
- [ ] Include Dijkstra, Greedy, and shortest-path concepts in the planner experience and explain why a station was selected.
- [ ] Provide trip planner inputs for starting point, destination, vehicle/range profile, and current battery level.
- [ ] Show a route map with nearby or along-route charging stations and visual route segments.
- [ ] Compare station distance, availability, connector compatibility, charging speed, estimated charging time, and route impact.
- [ ] Show a ranked best-station recommendation with a clear summary of why it was selected.
- [ ] Visualize route segments, charging stops, estimated arrival battery, and total journey time.
- [ ] Make station detail and comparison views responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
